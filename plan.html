Best time to visit

Jan to Mar

Hot, humid and rainy. Cyclones are most likely. Quietest and cheapest.

Apr to Jun

Temperatures ease and rain drops. A good balance of price and weather.

Jul to Sep

Coolest and driest. Great for hiking; evenings can be fresh.

Oct to Dec

Warming up and sunny. December is busy with holiday visitors.

Climate varies by region; the central plateau is cooler and wetter than the coast.
